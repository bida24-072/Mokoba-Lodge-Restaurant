The Restaurant

Simple food.
Honest flavours.

Our kitchen is led by Chef Kefilwe Mosarwa, who grew up cooking alongside her grandmother in Maun. Her menu is a love letter to Botswana — from slow-cooked seswaa to fresh Chobe bream, all sourced from local farmers, fishermen, and producers.

Whether you're dining indoors or under the stars on our open-air deck, every meal is prepared with intention, served with warmth, and paired with a wine list that celebrates Southern Africa.

Open daily: Breakfast 7–10am · Lunch 12–3pm · Dinner 6–9pm
