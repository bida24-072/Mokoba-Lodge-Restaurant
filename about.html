Our Story

A family-run lodge, built on a love for the wild and a deep respect for Botswana's people and land.

Since 2018

Born from a lifelong love of the bush.

Mokoba Lodge & Restaurant was founded by the Mokoba family — born in Kasane, raised on the edge of the wilderness, and driven by a simple vision: to share the beauty of their homeland with the world.

What began as a small family home with three guest rooms has grown into a seven-chalet lodge, a celebrated restaurant, and a basecamp for some of Botswana's most memorable safari experiences.

We are proudly Motswana-owned and operated. Every member of our team lives within 30km of the lodge. Every ingredient we serve is sourced from local farmers and fishermen wherever possible. Every rand that passes through our door feeds back into our community.

Our Values

What we stand for.

Hospitality. Guests arrive as visitors and leave as family. It's a Setswana tradition we take seriously.

Conservation. We operate on solar power, harvest rainwater, and support anti-poaching initiatives in the region.

Community. Over 90% of our staff are hired from nearby villages. We invest in local schools and skills training.

Craft. From the gin in your glass to the sheets on your bed — we choose quality, always. Even if it costs more.
