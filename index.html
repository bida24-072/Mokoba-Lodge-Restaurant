Welcome to Mokoba

A retreat crafted by nature.

Nestled on the edge of the wilderness, Mokoba Lodge & Restaurant is where Botswana's raw beauty meets thoughtful hospitality. Seven intimate chalets, a restaurant that celebrates local flavours, and experiences that connect you to the wild — all under an endless African sky.

Whether you're here for the safari, the sunsets, or the quiet luxury, Mokoba is your home in the bush.
